Turn the ideas and their keyword metrics into a weekly editorial plan.

1. Drop ideas whose keyword has no measurable demand, and say why in one line each.
2. Rank the rest by expected value: demand, difficulty and fit with the site.
3. For the top five, write a title, the main keyword with its metrics, an outline
   of four to six headings and one sentence on why it matters now.

Write in Markdown. Use only numbers present in the metrics; never invent them.
